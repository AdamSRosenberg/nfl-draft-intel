Signal Points
Beat reporters, Reddit, and 19 news outlets are scanned 3x daily. When a team is linked to a prospect, the article scores points:

● HIGH (3pts) — visited, trade up, targeting, priority
● MEDIUM (2pts) — fit, interest, mock, linked to
● LOW (1pt) — scouted, option, could be

Bonus pts if the team + player appear close together in the article.
Mock Draft Logic
Each pick uses a composite score blending two signals:

Consensus rank match — exact match to pick # = +50 bonus, 1 off = +20, within 3 = +5

Beat reporter signal — raw signal points linking that player to that team

The highest composite score wins the pick. Obvious locks (Mendoza #1) stay locked. Signal can move players up or down.
Confidence Levels
HIGH — consensus lock (exact rank match) or 15+ signal pts

MEDIUM — 8–14 signal pts, or within 3 picks of consensus rank

LOW — best available fallback, limited intel

Sources: 19 national outlets + 32 team feeds + Google News + Reddit — updated at 7am, 1pm & 7pm ET.

ⓘ “National Intel” means a prospect has buzz in national media but no specific team has been linked yet. It scores signal points but doesn't push a team pick.
